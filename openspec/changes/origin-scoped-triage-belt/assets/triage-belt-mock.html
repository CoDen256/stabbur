Prefilling

When primary providers run

On open, every empty field is filled the moment you land on a note. On button, nothing is looked up until you press ⚡ Fill all.

Only fill empty fields

A field you have typed into, or that arrived with the capture, is never overwritten by a prefill run.

Suggest panels start closed

Prefilled values are shown in the fields themselves; the provider rows stay out of the way until you open them.

Provider order drag to reorder · the first one that answers wins
